## Prioritized release actions

1. **Release branch and production**: obtain teammate review of [PR #151](https://github.com/CS3227-2610-MP4-CodeLeet/CS3227-2610-MP4/pull/151), reconcile `develop` with protected `master` through a reviewed release PR, run CI at the actual release head and confirm Vercel Production runs that SHA. The successful 10 November Production record is for the older `master` SHA, not proof that later changes are live.
2. **Hosted acceptance**: with synthetic accounts in the intended nonproduction or production test environment, record student and instructor sign-in/email recovery, Python/Java run and save, published cases, grade/XP, AI hints/advice, privacy denial, unavailable AI and quota behavior. Record URL, commit, browser, account role, fixture and outcome without secrets or real student data. Issue [#31](https://github.com/CS3227-2610-MP4-CodeLeet/CS3227-2610-MP4/issues/31) still tracks independent/hosted runner gates.
3. **Human academic checks**: each member reads and corrects their own reflection and contribution attribution, verifies generated logs, confirms the Canvas organization submission and rehearses the individual presentation. Freeze changes after the final submission as instructed by the course.

Passing CI, a protected branch or a published website narrows risk; none guarantees full marks or zero bugs. This review did not execute a new live AI or hosted end-to-end test.
